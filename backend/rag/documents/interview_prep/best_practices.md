# Interview Preparation Best Practices

## The STAR Method

Use the STAR method to structure behavioral interview answers:

### Structure
- **Situation**: Set the context. Describe the specific situation you were in.
- **Task**: Explain what you needed to accomplish. What was your responsibility?
- **Action**: Describe the specific actions YOU took. Use "I" not "we".
- **Result**: Share the outcome. Quantify results when possible (%, $, time saved).

### Example: "Tell me about a time you resolved a conflict in your team"
- **Situation**: "During a sprint planning at my previous company, two senior developers disagreed on the architecture for a new microservice."
- **Task**: "As the tech lead, I needed to align the team and make a decision without damaging team dynamics."
- **Action**: "I organized a 30-minute architecture review where each developer presented their approach with pros/cons. I facilitated the discussion, created evaluation criteria based on our requirements."
- **Result**: "We chose a hybrid approach that combined the best elements. Both developers felt heard, and we delivered the service 2 weeks ahead of schedule. The team's velocity improved by 15% in subsequent sprints."

## Common Behavioral Questions

### Leadership & Initiative
- Tell me about a time you led a project from start to finish
- Describe a situation where you had to make a difficult decision with limited information
- How have you mentored or helped develop a team member?

### Problem Solving
- Describe the most complex technical problem you've solved
- Tell me about a time when you had to debug a critical production issue
- How do you approach a problem you've never encountered before?

### Teamwork & Communication
- Tell me about a time you had to work with a difficult colleague
- Describe a situation where you had to explain a complex technical concept to a non-technical stakeholder
- How do you handle disagreements about technical decisions?

### Adaptability
- Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly
- Describe a situation where project requirements changed significantly
- How do you handle working under pressure or tight deadlines?

## Technical Interview Tips

### Coding Interviews
1. **Clarify the problem**: Ask questions before coding
2. **Think aloud**: Explain your thought process
3. **Start with brute force**: Then optimize
4. **Test your code**: Walk through examples, consider edge cases
5. **Analyze complexity**: Time and space complexity for each solution

### System Design Interviews
1. **Clarify requirements**: Functional and non-functional
2. **Estimate scale**: Users, QPS, storage, bandwidth
3. **High-level design**: Draw components and data flow
4. **Deep dive**: Focus on 2-3 components the interviewer cares about
5. **Trade-offs**: Discuss pros/cons of your choices

### Common Patterns
- **Two Pointers**: Array/string problems
- **Sliding Window**: Subarray/substring problems
- **Binary Search**: Sorted data, search space reduction
- **DFS/BFS**: Tree/graph traversal
- **Dynamic Programming**: Optimization, counting problems
- **Topological Sort**: Dependency ordering
